Three ways to touch one pattern

The same octagon–square construction under three mappings. Anchored is the recommendation: press near any star and drag toward or away from its centre, with a gain of 0.35 between finger and waist. Grab is the failed control condition: the waist follows the finger exactly, and you can feel how little travel that gives. Pad is the fallback. In Anchored and Grab, tapping a star cycles its ray pairing.

One-point family only. Tapping a star cycles which ray meets which, a simplification of Kaplan's inner-layer count. δ, rosettes, interlacing and irregular tiles are not built here, so this demonstrates the scalar geometry and the feel of three mappings, nothing more.

This is a standalone sketch with its own small contact-ray generator. It does not use the app's kernel in src/geometry/. It was first published alongside the September design analysis and is kept here so the comparison it supports stays reproducible. Open this file directly in a browser; it has no dependencies.
